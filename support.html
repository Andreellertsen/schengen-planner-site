Questions, feedback or found a bug? Email SUPPORT_EMAIL. Please include your device, iOS version and, if possible, the dates of the trips involved.

How does the 90/180 rule work?

Most visitors from outside the EU and EEA can stay in the Schengen area for up to 90 days in any 180-day period. On any given day, count the days you were in the Schengen area during the 180 days ending on that day. The days you enter and leave both count.

Why does the app say I can stay fewer days than I expected?

The app also protects your planned trips. If staying longer now would push a later trip over the limit, the longest stay is shortened. Open the trip to see the full preview.

Do days under a residence permit count?

Time in the country that issued your residence permit or long-stay (D) visa doesn't count toward your 90 days. Turn on "Residence Permit or D Visa" for those trips.

How do I move my trips to a new phone?

Go to Settings > Back Up Trips and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, choose Settings > Restore from Backup.

Is this an official tool?

No. Schengen Planner is a planning tool and isn't affiliated with the European Union or any government. Since April 2026, the EU's Entry/Exit System (EES) records your entries and exits at the border. Check important trips with the European Commission's official short-stay calculator.
