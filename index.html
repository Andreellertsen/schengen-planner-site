Schengen Planner

Plan trips, never overstay.

Schengen Planner helps you stay within the 90/180 rule when you travel to Europe. Add your trips and see how many days you've used, how many you have left, when days come back, and how long your next trip can be.

  • Live preview of how a trip affects your allowance before you save it
  • Planner for the longest stay and the earliest date you can go
  • Color calendar, allowance chart, reminders, widgets and a Live Activity
  • Separate travelers, residence permit periods, PDF and CSV export, and backups
  • No account, no ads, no tracking

Available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
