Privacy Policy

Effective December 2026

Schengen Planner doesn't collect any personal data.

Your data stays on your device

The trips, travelers and settings you enter are stored only on your device. They are shared with the app's own widgets on the same device, and nowhere else. The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Exports and backups

When you export a PDF or CSV file or create a backup, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to save or share it.

Siri and Shortcuts

When you ask Siri how many days you have left, the answer is calculated on your device from the trips you've entered.

Children

The app doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
